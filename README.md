## 📖 About MILO

Moving to a new city can be exciting, but settling into an unfamiliar place is not always easy.

Finding accommodation, discovering reliable local services, meeting new people and understanding the city can become overwhelming — especially when you don't know anyone there.

**MILO aims to change that.**

MILO is a community-powered city settlement platform designed to connect people moving to new cities with individuals and communities that share their hometown, language, culture or interests.

Imagine someone moving from Jaipur to Pune. Through MILO, they could discover people from Jaipur already living in Pune, connect with local communities, find a PG or flat, discover essential services and build meaningful friendships.

The idea is simple:

**You may be new to a city, but you don't have to start alone.**

---

## 🎯 The Problem We're Solving

People relocating to unfamiliar cities often face several challenges:

- **Social isolation:** Finding people with similar backgrounds and interests can be difficult.
- **Accommodation difficulties:** Finding trustworthy PGs, flats and compatible flatmates takes time.
- **Local discovery:** Newcomers often struggle to identify reliable tiffin services, mess facilities, laundry shops, groceries and other essentials.
- **Lack of local guidance:** People don't always know where to go, what to avoid or how things work in a new city.
- **Fragmented information:** Useful information is scattered across WhatsApp groups, social media, personal contacts and different platforms.

MILO aims to bring these experiences together in one community-driven platform.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

MILO creates a connected ecosystem where people can discover communities, find accommodation, explore essential local services and build relationships.

The platform is designed around four major pillars.

### 1. 👥 Community

Find people who share your hometown, language, culture or interests.

- Discover people from your hometown.
- Join language and cultural communities.
- Connect with residents already living in your destination city.
- Participate in community discussions.
- Share advice and experiences with newcomers.

### 2. 🏠 Living

Make finding a place to stay easier.

- Discover PG accommodations.
- Explore flats and rental listings.
- Find potential flatmates.
- Connect with property owners.
- Explore accommodation options based on location and preferences.

### 3. 📍 Local Discovery

Discover the services and facilities that make everyday life easier.

- Mess and tiffin services.
- Laundry services.
- Grocery stores.
- Transportation options.
- Healthcare facilities.
- Other useful local businesses and services.

### 4. 🤝 Connections

Build meaningful relationships in your new city.

- Discover people with shared backgrounds.
- Send connection requests.
- Build friendships.
- Communicate with community members.
- Find people willing to guide newcomers.

MILO has an India-wide vision, but the initial development and community-building efforts will focus on Pune and its surrounding areas.

### Initial Pilot Locations

- Pune
- Pimpri-Chinchwad
- Hinjewadi
- Wakad
- Akurdi
- Nigdi

Starting with a focused geographical area will help build active communities before expanding to other cities.

### Long-Term Vision

Expand MILO across major Indian cities and eventually create a connected network of communities throughout India.

## ⚙️ How MILO Will Work

A simplified user journey:

1. **Create an account:** Register and create a personal profile.
2. **Share your background:** Add your hometown, current city, languages and interests.
3. **Discover communities:** Find people and groups based on location and shared backgrounds.
4. **Connect with people:** Send connection requests and communicate.
5. **Explore living options:** Find PGs, flats and potential flatmates.
6. **Discover local services:** Find useful services around your new location.
7. **Settle in:** Build relationships and become part of your new community.

## 🔐 Privacy, Trust & Safety

Trust is essential for a community platform.

MILO's development will prioritize:

- Privacy-conscious user profiles.
- User-controlled visibility of personal information.
- No public display of exact residential addresses.
- Reporting and blocking mechanisms.
- Appropriate access controls for private conversations.
- Accommodation listing ownership checks.
- Clear distinctions between phone verification and identity verification.
- Secure authentication and database access policies.

The goal is to build a platform where people can connect while maintaining control over their personal information.

## 💻 Getting Started

Want to explore or contribute to MILO?

### Prerequisites

- Node.js
- npm
- Git

### 1. Clone the Repository

```bash
git clone https://github.com/Dheeraj47/milo-community-platform.git
```

### 2. Navigate to the Project

```bash
cd milo-community-platform
```

### 3. Install Dependencies

```bash
npm install
```

### 4. Start the Development Server

```bash
npm run dev
```

Open the local URL displayed in your terminal to view the application.

### 5. Build for Production

```bash
npm run build
```
